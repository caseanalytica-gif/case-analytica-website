How to mail a guide to someone in state prison

You can print an article from this site and mail it to someone in a New York State prison. DOCCS limits how much printed material can go in one letter, so the guides below were measured to fit.

The DOCCS rules

  • One piece of regular mail can hold no more than five pages of printed or photocopied material, including pages printed from the internet.
  • Don’t tape, glue or paste the pages together or to other papers.
  • Mail with printed pages in it can be held for up to six days while it goes through media review.
  • The return address has to be the person sending it. Mail from someone not named in the return address counts as third-party mail and is sent back.
  • A longer article can go in more than one envelope, five pages at a time.

Sources: DOCCS Directive 4572, Media Review (October 2, 2023), and Directive 4422, Incarcerated Individual Correspondence Program (July 28, 2021).

These are DOCCS rules for state prisons. They do not cover city or county jails, such as Rikers Island.

How to print one

  • Open the article and use your browser’s Print command. The printed copy leaves out the site’s menus, buttons and sign-up boxes.
  • If the print preview shows more than five pages, split it across two envelopes.
