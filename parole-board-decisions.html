How often the Parole Board says yes at a first interview.

In 2025 the Board of Parole approved 2,409 of its 5,672 first-interview decisions, or 42.5%. In 2008 it was 22.3%. These charts show every year in between, taken from the state’s own published data.

The agency last updated this data on 2026-04-23. Case Analytica read it on 2026-10-28. The figures stop at the end of 2025.

What this does not show

These are first-interview decisions only. They are not the Earned Eligibility Certificate denials this project reads (402 between October 2025 and March 2026, by the state prison system’s own count), and they say nothing about the reasons the Board gave. That count comes from a different source and is a different measure, so the two are not combined here.

Source and method

Data: Parole Board Decisions for Initial Interviews by Crime Type: Beginning 2008, published by the NYS Department of Corrections and Community Supervision on NY Open Data. 136,832 decisions counted. Initial parole-board interviews only, by calendar year of the decision. Not reappearances, and not limited to Earned Eligibility Certificate holders. The source records the decision in mixed upper and lower case, and spells “postponed” as “POSPONED” in 1,455 rows. Case Analytica counted those with the matching decisions and changed nothing else. The approval share is Case Analytica’s division of approved decisions by all decisions that year, and the source does not publish it. Cite the source, not this page.

Case Analytica refreshes this page by re-reading the source, not continuously. The date above is the only claim about how current it is. See how the parole data record is kept.
