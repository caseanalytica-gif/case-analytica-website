Brooklyn Criminal Court: Which Train Actually Goes There, and What to Expect

By Dean Mustaphalli·2026-09-19

Reflects law as of September 19, 2026

Read this part first

Brooklyn Criminal Court is 120 Schermerhorn Street. The court system's own page tells you to take the M train. The M does not stop there. Neither does the N, except late at night. Take the R, or the A, C or F to Jay Street.

Where it is

Brooklyn Criminal Court is at 120 Schermerhorn Street, near Livingston and Smith Streets, per the court system's own listing. One building, which makes Brooklyn simpler than Manhattan or the Bronx.

Getting there, and the listing that will strand you

The official directions say to take "the N, R or M train to the Lawrence Street Station." Two thirds of that is wrong in a way that matters on a weekday morning.

  • Lawrence Street–MetroTech is served by the R at all times. That is the train to take.
  • The N stops there late nights only. On a weekday morning it will not come.
  • The M does not serve that station at all. If you are standing on a platform waiting for the M, you are waiting for a train that is not coming.

What does work, all day:

  • R to Lawrence Street–MetroTech.
  • A, C or F to Jay Street–MetroTech.
  • 2, 3, 4 or 5 to Borough Hall.
  • G to Hoyt–Schermerhorn.
  • Bus: the B67, B41 or B45 to Livingston and Smith; the B63 or B65 to Atlantic and Smith.

The hours that matter

  • Arraignments: Monday through Sunday, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 a.m. Brooklyn runs a night part.
  • Desk Appearance Tickets, all other court parts, and the clerk's offices: Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., closed holidays.
  • General information: (347) 404-9400. Summons clerk: (646) 386-4869.

The exact question to ask

Before you leave the courthouse, ask the clerk or your attorney: "What is the next date, and which part is it in?" Write both down. If you are told only a date, that is an incomplete answer, and the part number is what tells you which floor to be on.

This page is about logistics only. Nothing here is advice about a specific case.
