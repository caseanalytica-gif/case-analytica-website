Manhattan Criminal Court: Which Building, Which Train, What to Expect

By Dean Mustaphalli·2026-09-19

Reflects law as of September 19, 2026

Read this part first

Manhattan criminal court runs out of two buildings four miles apart. Most cases are at 100 Centre Street. Some are at the Midtown Community Justice Center on West 54th Street. Before you travel, call (646) 386-4511 and confirm the address, not just the date.

Wrong borough? There is a guide for each one: all five New York City criminal courts, plus what to ask before the next date.

The two buildings

The court system lists two Manhattan locations, and they are nowhere near each other.

  • 100 Centre Street, one block from Worth Street, near Leonard and Franklin Streets. This is the main criminal court building and where arraignments happen.
  • Midtown Community Justice Center, 314 West 54th Street, between 8th and 9th Avenues. A separate problem-solving court, mostly for lower-level cases arising in Midtown.

If someone tells you "Midtown Community Court" and you go to Centre Street, you are four miles and a subway ride from where you are supposed to be. This is the single most common way a Manhattan court date goes wrong before it starts.

Getting there

For 100 Centre Street:

  • Subway: the 4, 5 or 6 to Brooklyn Bridge–City Hall is the closest. The 6, N, Q, R or W to Canal Street also works, as does the 1 to Franklin Street.
  • One correction to the official directions. The court system's own page lists the C train to Canal Street. The C does stop at a Canal Street station, but it is the one on Eighth Avenue, most of a mile west of the courthouse. If you are following that listing you will get off in the wrong place. Take a Lexington Avenue train instead.
  • Bus: the M1, M6 and M15 lines run nearby.

For Midtown Community Justice Center: the N, Q, R or W to 57th Street, or the A, C, D or 1 to Columbus Circle.

The hours that matter

  • Arraignments: Monday through Sunday, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 a.m. Manhattan runs a night part, so an evening arrest is often arraigned the same night.
  • Desk Appearance Tickets, all other court parts, and the clerk's offices: Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., closed holidays.
  • General information: (646) 386-4511. Summons clerk: (646) 386-4869 or (646) 386-4950.

Note the gap. Arraignments run until one in the morning, but if you need the clerk's office, a certificate of disposition, or any part other than arraignment, you are working inside a weekday nine to five.

The exact question to ask

Before you leave the courthouse, ask the clerk or your attorney: "What is the next date, which part is it in, and which building?" Write down all three. Manhattan runs criminal parts in two buildings four miles apart, and a next date with no address attached is how people end up in the wrong lobby.

This page is about logistics only. Nothing here is advice about a specific case.
