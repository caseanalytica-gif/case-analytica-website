Directory

Where to actually get help.

This site explains things. These are the people who do them. Public defense by borough, reentry and employment, help with a record, parole support, and the official tools for finding a court date or locating someone in custody.

Nothing here is an endorsement and none of these organisations are affiliated with Case Analytica. Every link was opened and checked on September 19, 2026. Eligibility rules vary, most have a geographic catchment, and a listing is not a promise that they can take your case. Call first.

Public defense, New York City

The Legal Aid Society

The largest public defender in the city. Criminal, civil, juvenile. Offices in all five boroughs.

Bronx Defenders

Holistic defense in the Bronx: criminal, family, immigration and civil under one roof.

Brooklyn Defender Services

Public defense in Brooklyn, with immigration and family practices alongside the criminal one.

Queens Defenders

Public defense in Queens, plus reentry and diversion programming.

New York County Defender Services

Public defense in Manhattan.

Neighborhood Defender Service of Harlem

Community-based holistic defense in Northern Manhattan.

Public defense, statewide

NYS Office of Indigent Legal Services

The state office overseeing public defense. Useful for finding who provides defense in a given county.

New York State Defenders Association

Statewide support for public defense, including a public defense backup center.

LawHelpNY

Finds free legal help by county and problem type. The best single starting point outside the city.

Reentry and employment

The Fortune Society

Reentry services: housing, employment, education, health, for people coming home.

Osborne Association

Reentry, family support for people with an incarcerated loved one, and services inside.

Center for Employment Opportunities

Immediate paid transitional work and job placement for people recently released.

Exodus Transitional Community

Reentry support in East Harlem, run largely by formerly incarcerated staff.

Getting Out and Staying Out

Reentry and employment for young men, ages 16 to 24.

Hour Children

Services for incarcerated and formerly incarcerated mothers and their children.

Records, rights and licensing

Legal Action Center

Legal help on criminal records, and on discrimination tied to a record, addiction or HIV status.

NYS DCJS record review

Request your own New York State criminal history record. This is how you find out what is actually on it.

Youth Represent

Legal help for young people affected by the justice system, including records and reentry.

Parole and people inside

Parole Preparation Project

Volunteers who help people serving life sentences prepare for parole board interviews.

Release Aging People in Prison

Works on parole release for older people and people serving long sentences.

Correctional Association of New York

The only independent organization with statutory authority to monitor New York prisons.

Immigration

Immigrant Defense Project

Specialists in the immigration consequences of a criminal case. Resources for defenders and families.

Courts and official

NY Courts: find a court

The court system's own locator, for addresses, parts and phone numbers.

NY Courts CourtHelp

Plain-language guides from the court system itself, including forms.

NYS DOCCS

Find someone in state custody, and the rules governing visits, mail and parole.

NYC Department of Correction inmate lookup

Find someone held in a New York City jail, including Rikers.
