📘 Free Guide

Beyond the free intake call

The free call maps out what's happening. Case navigation is someone in your corner for what comes next.

A single 20 to 30 minute call is enough to point you toward the right questions. It's not enough to sit with you through every court date, every piece of paperwork, and every decision point between arraignment and resolution. That's what case navigation is for.

What this is not

Case navigation is not legal advice and not legal representation. It does not replace an attorney, and nothing here should be read as instructions for a specific case. If you don't have an attorney, we'll help you find and connect with a licensed one or the right local resource. What this is: help understanding what's happening in plain language, organizing what to ask and when, and not facing the gaps in the system alone.

Just need somewhere to start? See help after an arrest in New York for the free first steps before deciding if ongoing navigation makes sense.
