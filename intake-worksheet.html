Get your facts in one place

Fill this in on your phone, or print it and write on it. It's for organizing what you already know before you talk to an attorney or book a call.

Nothing leaves this device unless you decide to send it

This worksheet doesn't submit anything on its own. What you type is saved on this device only, so you can close the page and come back to it. At the bottom there are two optional buttons if you decide you want to send it in: one sends it straight to us, the other opens your own email app so you can read it over first. Neither one does anything unless you tap it.

Do not write down what happened. Stick to what's on the paperwork: names, dates, charges as they're written, court and part. The account of events belongs in a conversation with a licensed attorney, not on a phone or a piece of paper.
