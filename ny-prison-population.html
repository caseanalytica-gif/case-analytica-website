New York State prison population by year, race and age

On March 31, 2026, 33,884 people were in the custody of the state prison system. On March 31, 2008, it was 62,597.

Source: Incarcerated Individuals Under Custody: Beginning 2008, published by the NYS Department of Corrections and Community Supervision. The agency last updated it on 2026-07-30. Case Analytica read it on 2026-10-31. This is a count on one day each year, not a count over the year.

Population is not admissions

The count above is everyone in custody on one day. A different measure, admissions, counts how many people entered state prison during a year, and someone admitted twice is counted twice. The two are never added together. Admissions by year are on the data hub.
