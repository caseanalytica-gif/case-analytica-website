# 15: Project Reset: How a Desk Appearance Ticket Can Avoid a Criminal Record

**Format:** Short · ~75 sec | **Pillar:** Restorative Justice | **Slot:** Week 5 (new batch, unscheduled)

## Script

**Hook (0:00)**
A young man once showed me a Desk Appearance Ticket and asked what happens next. His lawyer told him to just show up on the date. Nobody mentioned the program that could have made his case disappear before that date ever mattered.

**Body (0:08)**
It's called Project Reset, and it runs through the district attorney's office in each of the five boroughs. If you got a Desk Appearance Ticket, that's an appearance ticket under Criminal Procedure Law 150.10, for a low-level, non-violent misdemeanor, you may qualify to complete a short community-based program instead of ever going to court. Two to four hours, workshops or counseling. Complete it, and the DA declines to prosecute. No conviction. No trial. Arrest record sealed. This isn't a fringe program: over 10,000 people have avoided a criminal record through it.

**Self-advocacy line (0:45)**
Nobody is required to tell you this exists. It has to be raised, by you, the same day you get that ticket, not the week of your court date. Ask directly: "Am I eligible for Project Reset or an equivalent diversion program in this borough?"

**CTA (1:05)**
This is general information, not legal advice for your specific ticket. Link in bio if you or someone you love just got a DAT and needs help figuring out what's actually on the table.
