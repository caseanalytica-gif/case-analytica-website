Parole

In 2025 the Board of Parole approved 2,409 of its 5,672 first-interview decisions, or 42.5%. In 2008 it was 22.3%.

Source: Parole Board Decisions for Initial Interviews by Crime Type: Beginning 2008, published by the NYS Department of Corrections and Community Supervision. The agency last updated it on 2026-04-23. Case Analytica read it on 2026-10-31. First interviews only. These are not the Earned Eligibility Certificate denials the parole reporting project reads.

State prisons

Admissions per year

The state prison system admitted 10,125 people in 2025, down from 26,063 in 2008. In 2025, 8,751 were new court commitments and 1,374 were people returned for a parole violation.

Source: Prison Admissions: Beginning 2008, published by the NYS Department of Corrections and Community Supervision. The agency last updated it on 2026-02-26. Case Analytica read it on 2026-10-31. This counts admissions, not people: someone admitted twice in a year is counted twice. The agency says its county field should not be used to count convictions by county.

The county rows add up to 404,985 adult arrest events in 2025, and 388,330 in 2024. In 2025, 152,710 were counted as felonies and 252,275 as misdemeanors. The five New York City counties account for 227,498.

Source: Adult Arrests 18 and Older by County: Beginning 1970, published by the NYS Division of Criminal Justice Services. The agency last updated it on 2026-09-22. Case Analytica read it on 2026-10-31. Case Analytica added up the county rows to get a state figure. Arrests of people under 18 and non-fingerprintable offenses are not included.

New York City jobs and records data

How this data is kept

  • Each figure is read at the agency that publishes it and stated as the agency states it.
  • Every indicator shows who published it, when the agency last updated it, and the day we read it.
  • Different measures stay separate. Admissions over a year and custody on one day are both prison counts, but they are not the same thing and are never combined.
  • We refresh by re-reading the source, not continuously. The read date is the only claim about how current a figure is.
  • To cite a figure, cite the agency named under it. This page is an index.

Known gaps

No public count of convictions sealed under the Clean Slate Act has been published, so it is not here (see the jobs and records page). No public file gives sentence structure, such as the minimum term, so the number of people covered by Correction Law 805 cannot be counted from open data.
