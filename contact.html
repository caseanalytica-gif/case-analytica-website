Send a tip, or talk through where a case stands

Two different things, so they are kept apart here.

Send a tip

If you know something about New York's courts, jails, prisons or parole system that should be reported, tell us. Documents help. Say how you know what you know.

Don't send the details of your own open case. Those belong with a licensed attorney.

Email a tip to caseanalytica@gmail.com

Talk through a case

A 20–30 minute conversation to understand where a case actually stands: what stage it's at, what the realistic timeline looks like, and what to ask for next. If it's a legal question that needs an attorney, we'll help point you to one. If it's a question about the process, reentry support, or a restorative justice option, we'll walk through what's actually available in your county.
