Two people can walk out of the same courthouse, over the same relationship, holding two functionally different pieces of paper: one from Family Court, one out of a criminal part. Most people never find out which one they're holding, what it actually restricts, or that violating it is its own separate crime stacked on top of whatever case is already pending. Read the order, because what it restricts is not always what you'd assume.
