Two people can walk out of the same courthouse, over the same relationship, holding two completely different orders of protection: one from Family Court, one out of a criminal part. Most never find out which one they're holding, or that breaking a criminal court order under CPL 530.12 is its own separate crime on top of whatever case is already pending.
