Right before a felony guilty plea in New York, a judge reads one specific line into the record: a warning about deportation. Most defendants nod and move on because by that point everyone in the room just wants the hearing over. That single sentence, read once, with no requirement that anyone confirm you actually understood it, can be the most consequential thing you hear in the entire case.
