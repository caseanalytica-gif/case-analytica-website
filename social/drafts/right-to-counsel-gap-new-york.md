Ask most people when the right to a lawyer kicks in after a New York arrest, and you'll hear "immediately." The honest answer: New York guarantees a lawyer at arraignment, not before it, and in the hours between arrest and that first court appearance, a person can be questioned and held with no attorney in the room at all.
