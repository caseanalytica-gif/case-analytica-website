New York guarantees a lawyer at arraignment. Not before it. In the hours between arrest and that first court appearance, a person can be questioned, processed, and held with no attorney in the picture at all unless they know to ask. For low-income defendants, that gap is exactly when police questioning happens and exactly when a case can get shaped before a defense attorney ever sees it.
