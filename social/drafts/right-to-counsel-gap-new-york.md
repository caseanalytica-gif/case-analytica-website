Ask most people when the right to a lawyer kicks in after a New York arrest, and you'll hear some version of immediately, or as soon as you ask for one. The honest answer is more specific, and more uncomfortable: the state guarantees a lawyer at arraignment, not necessarily before it. In the hours in between, a person can be questioned and held with no attorney in the picture at all.
