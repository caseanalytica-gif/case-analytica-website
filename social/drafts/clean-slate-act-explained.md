New York started automatically sealing eligible criminal records back in November 2024. Most people who qualify still don't know it, and most people who've heard of Clean Slate don't know whether their specific record actually meets the criteria. A misdemeanor can seal 3 years after release, a felony 8 years, but the clock doesn't wait for parole or probation to end, and a pending charge or ongoing supervision can hold up the sealing even after time runs out.
