Nobody posts a price list for a criminal defense lawyer in New York, and calling around for quotes while you're in the middle of a case is exhausting on its own. Misdemeanor retainers usually run $2,500 to $7,500, felony retainers start around $5,000 to $10,000 and can climb past $50,000, and the question to ask before signing anything is whether that fee covers the whole case or stops before trial.
