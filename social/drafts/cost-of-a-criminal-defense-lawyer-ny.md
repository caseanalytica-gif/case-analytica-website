Nobody posts a price list for a criminal defense lawyer in New York. Felony retainers can start around $5,000 and climb past $50,000, and a huge share of sticker shock comes from a retainer that only covered pretrial work, with a separate, much larger fee kicking in if the case goes to trial. Ask what the fee actually covers before signing anything.
