Nobody posts a price list for a criminal defense lawyer in New York. Misdemeanor retainers tend to run $2,500 to $7,500, felony retainers start around $5,000 to $10,000 and climb well past $50,000 once a charge gets serious. Before you sign anything, ask whether that number covers the whole case or only the work up to trial.
