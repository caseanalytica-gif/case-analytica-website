A New York woman fled an abusive ex, struck and injured a bystander in the process, and faced a maximum of 25 years. The prosecution's plea offer came with a condition: give up her hearing under the Domestic Violence Survivors Justice Act. Three years and two appeals later, the Court of Appeals said that condition should never have been on the table.
