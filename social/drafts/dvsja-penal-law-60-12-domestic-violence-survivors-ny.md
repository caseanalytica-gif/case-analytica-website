A woman fleeing an abusive ex was facing 25 years for attempted murder. Her lawyer asked for the hearing New York built exactly for her situation, Penal Law 60.12. The prosecution's plea offer required her to give that hearing up first. She took it, and three years and two appeals later, the Court of Appeals said that condition should never have been on the table.
