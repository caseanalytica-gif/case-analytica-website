A sentence ends. The conviction doesn't. A Certificate of Relief from Disabilities under Correction Law Article 23 doesn't erase a conviction, but it removes the automatic legal bar that blocks a job or license because of one. Most people never hear the phrase until they've already lost the opportunity it could have saved.
