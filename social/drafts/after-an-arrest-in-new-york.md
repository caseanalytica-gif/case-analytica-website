If someone you love was just arrested in New York, you probably have a hundred questions and nobody explaining them in order. Here's one worth asking before any plea discussion starts: what are all the ways this case can end without a conviction? Not what the offer is. All the ways: diversion, a restorative justice program, an ACD, a dismissal on a speedy trial motion. The offer on the table is one path, and it gets mentioned first because it's the one that closes the file.
