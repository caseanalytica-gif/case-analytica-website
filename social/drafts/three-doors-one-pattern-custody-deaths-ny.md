## LinkedIn

**New York paid $24 million for Robert Brooks. The same week, a 20-year-old died in a Queens courthouse cell.**

---

On September 16, Luis Alberto Lopez Castillo was found in medical distress at Queens Criminal Court, shortly after he saw a judge. He died at Jamaica Hospital. He is the eighth person to die in city Department of Correction custody this year. He had been held since last October and convicted of nothing.

Five days later, Robert Brooks' family announced a $24 million settlement with the state. Officers beat him to death in the Marcy infirmary in December 2024. His family's attorney says it is thought to be the largest prison death settlement in the country.

The third item that week was a one-hour Zoom. The Center for Justice Innovation is hosting a webinar on October 14 about housing people who move through courts and jails, instead of releasing them at night with a MetroCard and no coat.

I put the three side by side because they share a timing problem. The settlement, the oversight law passed after Brooks, the medical examiner's ruling in Queens: all of it comes after the door has closed. The places where harm could be stopped earlier, the holding room behind arraignments, a courthouse with no medical staff, a place to sleep on release, get the least money and the least attention.

The piece includes a New York City public defender's video from inside that holding area and WKTV's report from the Brooks press conference.
