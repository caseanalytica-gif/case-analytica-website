Jose Medina was 68, missing most of both lungs to cancer surgery, and dependent on a breathing machine when New York Focus profiled him in 2022. He'd applied for medical parole twice, in 2006 and again in 2020, and been denied both times with nothing more than a form letter calling him "not an appropriate candidate." New York has a real law that lets someone terminally ill or severely debilitated come home. Almost nobody who needs to hear about it ever does.
