A man missing most of both lungs to cancer surgery applied for medical parole in New York. Denied, with no explanation beyond a form letter saying he was not an appropriate candidate. He applied again during Covid. Denied again, same form letter. New York has a real law for exactly this situation. Almost nobody who needs to hear about it ever does.
