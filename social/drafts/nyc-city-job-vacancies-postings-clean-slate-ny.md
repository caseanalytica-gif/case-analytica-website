## LinkedIn

**New York City government had 15,530 empty positions in April. Its job board listed 3,821 on October 6.**

---

The first number is the Comptroller's count of vacant full-time positions. The second comes from the city's own Jobs NYC postings data: 1,384 postings covering 3,821 positions. They measure different things, and the gap alone proves nothing. But the job board is what a job seeker can see.

For someone with a conviction record, there is a second gap. The Clean Slate Act took effect November 16, 2024. The courts have until November 16, 2027 to set up automatic sealing, and the state has not published how many convictions have been sealed so far.

Private employers in the city added 27,600 jobs between August 2025 and August 2026 by the BLS count, an increase of under 1 percent, and the August figure is preliminary.

We are keeping these figures in one place, with sources and dates, on a new jobs and records data page, and will ask every month for the Clean Slate sealing count.
