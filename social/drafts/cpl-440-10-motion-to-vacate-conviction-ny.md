People find out too late, and almost always the same way: years into a sentence, someone finally hears there was a document they could have filed to challenge the conviction. CPL 440.10 lets a New York court vacate a judgment for reasons that never made it onto the record, but it comes with procedural traps that can shut the door for good if you miss them.
