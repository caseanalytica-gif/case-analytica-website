New York does not expunge convictions, not for anyone, under any statute, except one narrow marijuana carve-out. What CPL 160.59 actually offers is sealing: discretionary, filed by motion, available ten years after sentencing or release, whichever is later. And under 160.59(11), no prosecutor and no plea agreement can make you sign away that right ahead of time.
