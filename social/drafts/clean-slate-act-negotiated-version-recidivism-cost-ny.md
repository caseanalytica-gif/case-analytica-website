New York's Clean Slate Act got negotiated down before it ever reached Hochul's desk. The felony wait grew from seven years to eight, and the clock moved to start at release instead of sentencing, which for anyone who actually served time stretches the real wait well past eight years. The courts don't even have to finish building the system that seals those records until November 2027.
