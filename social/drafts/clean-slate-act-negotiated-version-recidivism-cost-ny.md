New York's Clean Slate Act didn't get watered down by some backroom amendment after it passed. It was negotiated down before the vote, and advocates said so at the time. Governor Hochul signed it in November 2023, then gave the courts until 2027 to actually build the system that seals a qualifying record. Every year between now and then is a year someone qualifies on paper and still can't prove it to a landlord or an employer.
