## LinkedIn

**I went to the city's snow shoveler sign-up in Queens. Nobody asked about a record.**

---

On Saturday, October 10, the Department of Sanitation held an emergency snow shoveler registration at PS 40 in Jamaica. The paperwork was a W-4, a state withholding form, a Social Security card and proof of address. There was no job application, no fingerprinting and no question about arrests or convictions.

Under Clean Slate, a misdemeanor becomes eligible to seal three years after sentencing or release, and a felony after eight. The courts have until November 16, 2027 to finish sealing. Until then, a conviction that qualifies can still come up on a background check, and for most city jobs that check comes after the offer.

The pay went up this year to $30 an hour, $45 after 40 hours in a week. It is per diem work, called in when it snows. Register before November 30 for a paid orientation and a paycheck within two weeks of working.

The article has the requirements, the documents to bring, the next sign-up dates in every borough, and the one question to ask at the table.
