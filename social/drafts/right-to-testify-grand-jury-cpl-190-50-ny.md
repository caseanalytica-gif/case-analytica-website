Most people learn what an indictment is at the exact moment it lands on them, after the one window to testify in front of the grand jury has already closed. The moment you're arraigned on a felony complaint, ask whether the DA has filed notice of intent to present the case to a grand jury, and whether notice has been served that you want to testify.
