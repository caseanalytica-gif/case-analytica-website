There is no constitutional right to testify before a New York grand jury. There's a statutory one, CPL 190.50, and it runs out the moment an indictment is filed. Miss the written notice and a five-day deadline is all that's left to challenge it, and that clock runs whether or not anyone realized the right was violated in the first place.
