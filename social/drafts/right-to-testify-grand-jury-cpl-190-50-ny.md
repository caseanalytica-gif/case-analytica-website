The moment you're arraigned on a felony complaint in New York, ask: has the DA filed notice of intent to present this case to a grand jury, and have we served written notice that I want to testify. Under CPL 190.50 that window closes the day an indictment is filed, and unlike most of what happens after arraignment, it does not come back once it's gone.
