Most people learn what an indictment is at the exact moment it lands on them, and by then they've already missed the one window where they could have testified before the grand jury themselves. Nobody explains that window in advance. It closes the moment the indictment is filed, and unlike almost everything else after arraignment, your attorney cannot revive it once it's gone.
