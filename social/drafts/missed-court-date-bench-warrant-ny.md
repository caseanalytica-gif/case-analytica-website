Missing a court date is not, by itself, its own crime in New York. A bench warrant can be issued the same day, but the bail jumping statutes only kick in if you don't appear, voluntarily, within thirty days of the date you missed. That thirty days is a statutory element of the charge, not a grace period a judge hands out.
