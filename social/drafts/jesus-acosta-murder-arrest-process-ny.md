Jesus Acosta was released on parole in April 2026 after serving more than a decade in state prison. Four months later, he's charged with two counts of murder and two counts of attempted murder, and he's facing a parole violation hold running on a separate track from the new case entirely. His arrest and arraignment are a real window into how New York's diversion programs work, and exactly where they stop applying once the charges get this serious.
