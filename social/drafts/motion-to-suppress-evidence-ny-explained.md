A plea offer is priced on the strength of the evidence against you. If a search was illegal, a confession was coerced, or a lineup was staged without a lawyer there, that evidence can be thrown out before a jury ever sees it. None of that happens automatically. The motion actually has to get filed, and skipping it is one of the quietest ways a case gets settled on the state's terms instead of the facts.
