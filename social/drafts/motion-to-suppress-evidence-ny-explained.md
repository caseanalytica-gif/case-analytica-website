Ask most people what happens between an arrest and a plea, and they'll describe the offer. Almost nobody describes the fight that's supposed to happen first. Before a case ever reaches a plea, New York law lets a defendant challenge how the evidence against them was obtained: an illegal search, a coerced confession, a staged lineup. Win that motion and the case the offer was priced on can fall apart before a jury ever sees it.
