A domestic violence survivor gets arrested for something that grew directly out of surviving the abuse that put her there in the first place. A prosecutor puts a plea deal on the table, and buried inside it is a condition: sign away the hearing that could cut the sentence down to a fraction of what's being threatened, or walk away from the deal entirely. In April 2026, New York's highest court said that trade is not legal. The hearing was never a chip prosecutors were allowed to bargain with.
