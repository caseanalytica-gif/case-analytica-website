A prosecutor put a plea deal in front of a domestic violence survivor with a condition attached: sign away the hearing that could cut her sentence to a fraction of what she was facing, or the deal was off. In April 2026, New York's highest court said that trade was never legal. The Penal Law 60.12 hearing was never a chip prosecutors got to bargain with.
