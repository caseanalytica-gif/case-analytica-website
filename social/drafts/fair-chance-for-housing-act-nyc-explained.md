A conviction from eight years ago used to be enough for a New York City landlord to reject your application before anyone looked at your income or your references. Since January 2025, that's not allowed in most cases. If an offer gets pulled after a background check, ask to see the written Fair Chance Housing Notice and the specific reason tied to a legitimate business interest. If a landlord can't produce that, the denial itself may be the violation.
