A conviction from eight years ago shouldn't be the reason an apartment application gets thrown out before anyone even looks at your income or references. Since January 2025, in most of New York City, it isn't allowed to be. The Fair Chance for Housing Act requires a conditional offer before a landlord can even start a background check.
