Most of what gets handed over in discovery now isn't a manila folder, it's a login, and the platform behind that login keeps a second file nobody asked for: how many times a defense account opened each piece of evidence, and for how long. That access log isn't evidence in the case. It's a record of how the defense is thinking about the case, and New York's work-product rule was written before anyone thought to ask whether that counts.
