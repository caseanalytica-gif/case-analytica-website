People ask me some version of this constantly: it's been months, sometimes over a year, and their case still hasn't gone anywhere. CPL 30.30 doesn't require a trial by a certain date. It requires the prosecution to be ready for trial within a set window, and "ready" and "went to trial" are not the same event.
