People ask me some version of this constantly: it's been months, sometimes over a year, and their case hasn't moved. Is there a law that forces it? There is, but CPL 30.30 sets a deadline for when prosecutors have to say they're ready for trial, not a deadline for the trial itself, and that difference is exactly what most people get wrong.
