New York's speedy trial statute doesn't promise you a trial by a deadline. It only requires prosecutors to say they're ready for one within a set window, and "ready for trial" and "actually went to trial" are not the same event. Ask your attorney for the actual CPL 30.30 date on your case right now, after excludable time, and ask to see how they calculated it.
