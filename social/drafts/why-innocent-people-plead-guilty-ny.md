97 percent of felony convictions in large urban courts come from a guilty plea, not a trial. That number isn't a fact of nature. It's the output of overcharging, bail pressure, and a system where every professional in the room, prosecutor, defense counsel, and judge, has an incentive to see the case settle rather than go to trial.
