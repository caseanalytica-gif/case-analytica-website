Nationally, 97 percent of felony convictions in large urban courts come from a guilty plea, not a trial, according to the Bureau of Justice Statistics. A Stanford Law Review study of misdemeanor cases in Harris County found people held pretrial were 25 percent more likely to plead guilty than similarly situated defendants who were released: same facts, same charge, same evidence. Here's the pressure behind that number, and why some share of the people inside it are innocent of what they pleaded to.
