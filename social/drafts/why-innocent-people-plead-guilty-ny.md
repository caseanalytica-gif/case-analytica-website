97 percent of felony convictions in large urban courts come from a guilty plea, not a trial, per the Bureau of Justice Statistics. A Stanford Law Review study found defendants held in pretrial detention were 25 percent more likely to plead guilty than similarly situated defendants who were released, same facts, same charge, same evidence. The plea isn't a verdict. It's a bet on exhaustion.
