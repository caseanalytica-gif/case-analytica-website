Raise the Age decides which court hears a teenager's case. Youthful Offender status under CPL Article 720 decides what happens to the record once that case ends. People mix the two up constantly, and that mistake is exactly how someone who was entitled to a sealed record ends up with a permanent felony conviction instead.
