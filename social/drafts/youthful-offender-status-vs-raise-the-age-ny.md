Raise the Age decides which court hears a teenager's case. Youthful Offender status under CPL Article 720 decides what happens to that same person's record once the case ends. People treat those as the same thing constantly, and that mix-up is exactly how someone eligible for a sealed record ends up with a permanent conviction instead.
