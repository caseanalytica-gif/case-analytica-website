I went through the public protection budget bills New York enacted in 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026, looking for anything that changed who gets diverted from jail to treatment.

The money: the 2023 budget doubled alternatives-to-incarceration funding to $31.4 million, according to the governor's office and Vera. The Fortune Society reports this year's budget kept it at $31.42 million. In March, the Legislature's proposals had included tens of millions more for violence prevention and ATI programs, New York Focus reported.

The law: none of the four bills' Part summaries lists a change to the judicial diversion statute, CPL Article 216. The one diversion change was Part GG of this year's bill, which repealed a rule about where a transferred mental health court case goes if the defendant fails the program.

The bill that would change who qualifies, the Treatment Court Expansion Act, sits outside the budget. It was struck and refiled in the Senate on September 23 without a floor vote.
