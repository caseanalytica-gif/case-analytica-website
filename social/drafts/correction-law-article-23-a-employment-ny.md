If you're turned down for a job in New York after a conditional offer, ask directly: can I get the written statement of reasons under Correction Law 754, and did you factor in a certificate of relief or good conduct if I have one. That question alone tells you whether the employer actually ran the Article 23-A analysis the law requires, or just said no and moved on.
