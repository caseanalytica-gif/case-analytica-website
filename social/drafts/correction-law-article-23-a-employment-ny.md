A conviction on a background check is not, by itself, a legal reason to turn someone away from a job in New York. An employer has to show either a direct relationship between the conviction and the job's duties or an unreasonable risk to safety or property. Ask for the written statement of reasons under Correction Law 754. Most people who get turned down never do.
