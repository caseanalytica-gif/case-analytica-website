New York publishes two death counts for 2026, and neither says why people died.

The city count is nine, as of November 10. The state's page lists 149 names through November 6, each with a birth date, ID number, date of death and prison, and no cause. All of 2024 was 144.

I put the counts in one place with the staffing numbers and the parole numbers, and tied each figure to its source. No source links a parole denial to a death, and the piece says so. It also says what the numbers can't support: Rikers is a jail and the state prisons hold sentenced people, and the two agencies count differently.
