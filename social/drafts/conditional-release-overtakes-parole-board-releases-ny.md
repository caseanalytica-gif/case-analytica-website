## LinkedIn

**On a 3-to-9 sentence in New York, a parole denial can turn 3 years inside into 6.**

---

That is why Case Analytica is documenting what the Parole Board writes when it denies people who hold an Earned Eligibility Certificate. Nobody publishes it.

Conditional release is now about 43% of New York prison releases and parole about 41%, by the Correctional Association of New York's count. In 2019 parole was about half.

The law sets the gap. The minimum on most felony sentences is at most a third of the maximum. Conditional release comes at two-thirds. A person the Board keeps denying serves twice as long, and the Less Is More Act's earned time can only cut the supervision left after that.

The state has a rule meant to prevent this for people who do the work. Correction Law 805 says a person holding an Earned Eligibility Certificate "shall be granted parole release" at the minimum unless the Board makes two findings. DOCCS's own report shows 402 certificate interviews from October 2025 to March 2026 where the Board did not approve release.
