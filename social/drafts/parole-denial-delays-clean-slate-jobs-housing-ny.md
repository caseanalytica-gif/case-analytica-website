## LinkedIn

**A parole denial in New York adds time inside, and then it pushes back everything that comes after.**

---

Correction Law 805 says a person holding an Earned Eligibility Certificate "shall be granted parole release" unless the Board makes two findings. From October 2025 to March 2026, DOCCS counted 402 certificate interviews where the Board said no.

Take a 3-to-9 sentence. Paroled at the first hearing, a person is home at year 3. Denied every time, they come home at conditional release, year 6. Clean Slate seals a felony only after eight years have passed since release, so the record that could have sealed at year 11 waits until year 14.

Those are three more years of background checks. The State Comptroller found that 45% of the people released from New York prisons in 2024 had no permanent residence. Prison Policy Initiative research ties homelessness and unemployment after release to going back.

Case Analytica is reading what the Board writes in these denials and checking it against the standard the law sets. Readers fund that work, and gifts are tax-deductible.
