Someone got ten years on a drug charge that could have gone through judicial diversion instead. Not the lawyer, not the DA, not the judge, nobody in the room ever raised it. Under CPL Article 216 a judge can send an eligible felony drug case to treatment even over a prosecutor's objection, but that only happens if someone actually asks before the plea gets signed.
