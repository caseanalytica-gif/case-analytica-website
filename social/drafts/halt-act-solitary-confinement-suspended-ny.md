New York passed a law in 2021 that caps how many days in a row someone can be locked in a cell alone. In February 2025, the agency that runs the state's prisons decided on its own that it didn't have to follow that cap. More than a year later, the lawsuit over that decision is still open in an Albany County courtroom, and the cap still isn't being enforced the way the law describes it.
