New York capped solitary confinement at 15 consecutive days under the HALT Act in 2021. In February 2025, the state's own prison agency suspended it anyway, as part of the deal that ended an unauthorized officer walkout. The lawsuit over that suspension, Smalls v. Martuscello, is still open in an Albany County courtroom.
