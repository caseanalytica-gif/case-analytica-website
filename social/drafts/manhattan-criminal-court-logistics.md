## LinkedIn

**Manhattan criminal court is two buildings four miles apart. Being sent to the wrong one costs you the morning.**

---

Most Manhattan criminal cases are heard at 100 Centre Street, a block from Worth Street. Some are at the Midtown Community Justice Center, 314 West 54th Street, between 8th and 9th. The court system lists both under the same borough.

If somebody says "Midtown Community Court" and you go to Centre Street, you are four miles and a subway ride from where you are supposed to be, on a morning when being late has consequences.

There is a second problem on the same page. The official directions tell you to take the C train to Canal Street. The C does stop at a Canal Street station, but it is the one on Eighth Avenue, most of a mile west of 100 Centre Street. Take a Lexington Avenue train to Brooklyn Bridge or Canal instead.

This is the third wrong subway listing I have found on the court system's own location pages while writing these borough guides. The Bronx page lists the C to 161st Street, and the C has not run to the Bronx in years. The Brooklyn page names the M to Lawrence Street, and the M does not serve that station at all.

None of it is a scandal. It is stale copy on a government page that almost nobody has reason to check, and it costs a missed calendar call, which can become a bench warrant.

The article has both addresses, the phone number to confirm which building, the arraignment hours, and the clerk's office hours, which close earlier than the arraignment part does.

That completes all five boroughs.

Most cases are at 100 Centre Street. Some are at the Midtown Community Justice Center on West 54th Street. Both are Manhattan criminal court, and they are nowhere near each other.

So if you are told "Midtown" and you show up at Centre Street, you are across town on a morning when being late matters.

One more thing worth knowing. The court's own website tells you to take the C to Canal Street. That is the Canal Street station on Eighth Avenue, most of a mile from the courthouse. Take the 4, 5 or 6 to Brooklyn Bridge, or the 6 to Canal, instead.

Before you leave the house, call (646) 386-4511 and confirm the building, not just the date.

Full page has both addresses, the hours, and the trains that actually get you there. This one finishes all five boroughs.
