## LinkedIn

**The court system's own directions to Brooklyn Criminal Court name a train that does not stop there.**

---

Brooklyn Criminal Court is at 120 Schermerhorn Street. The Unified Court System's location page tells you to take "the N, R or M train to the Lawrence Street Station."

Two thirds of that is wrong in a way that matters at 8:30 on a weekday morning.

Lawrence Street–MetroTech is served by the R at all times. The N serves it late nights only. The M does not serve that station at all. Someone following the official directions on the way to a court date is standing on a platform waiting for a train that is not coming, watching the clock.

This is the third wrong subway listing I have found on the court system's own pages while writing these borough guides. The Bronx page lists the C train to 161st Street–Yankee Stadium; the C has not run to the Bronx in years. The Manhattan page sends you to Canal Street on the C, which is the Eighth Avenue station, most of a mile from 100 Centre Street.

None of this is anyone's scandal. It is stale copy on a government page that almost nobody has reason to check. It costs a missed calendar call, and a missed calendar call can become a bench warrant.

What actually works, all day: the R to Lawrence Street, the A, C or F to Jay Street, the 2, 3, 4 or 5 to Borough Hall, or the G to Hoyt–Schermerhorn. The article has the bus lines, the arraignment hours, and the clerk's office hours, which close earlier than the arraignment part.

## Facebook

**Going to Brooklyn Criminal Court? The official directions tell you to take the M. The M does not stop there.**

---

Brooklyn Criminal Court is 120 Schermerhorn Street. The court system's own website says to take the N, R or M to Lawrence Street.

The R goes there all day. The N only goes there late at night. The M does not go there at all.

So if you are heading to a court date on a weekday morning and you follow the official directions, you could be standing on a platform waiting for a train that is never coming. Miss the calendar call and you can end up with a bench warrant over a subway listing.

Take the R to Lawrence Street. The A, C or F to Jay Street works too, so does the 2, 3, 4 or 5 to Borough Hall, or the G to Hoyt–Schermerhorn.

Arraignments there run every day from 9 a.m. to 1 a.m. The clerk's office is weekdays only, 9 to 5. Those are different hours and people mix them up constantly.

Full page has the bus lines and the phone numbers worth having before you travel.

## Note on tone

The article and this copy both stop short of blaming anyone for the wrong listing. It reads as
stale copy on a government page, not negligence, and the point is the consequence for a person
travelling to court rather than the mistake itself. Worth keeping that framing if you edit. It
is also the third such error found, so the pattern is worth naming once without turning it into
an accusation.
