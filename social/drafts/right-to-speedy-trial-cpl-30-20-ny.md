"Speedy trial" sounds like a countdown clock that ends the case once it hits zero. New York's constitutional right under CPL 30.20 doesn't work that way at all. Courts weigh it with a five-factor balancing test, and a "serious" charge can count against you even when the delay was long and the prosecution's fault. Are you tracking the right clock, or the wrong one?
