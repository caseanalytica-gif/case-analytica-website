If a case has been sitting for months with no clear next step, ask your attorney directly: are we tracking this under the CPL 30.30 statutory clock, the constitutional Taranovich claim, or both, and what does the delay actually look like under each one. They are two separate motions with two separate standards, and a case can be weak on one while still worth raising on the other.
