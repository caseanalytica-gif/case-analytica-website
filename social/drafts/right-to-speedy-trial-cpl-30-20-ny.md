New York guarantees you a speedy trial under CPL 30.20, but there's no fixed number of days attached to it. Courts weigh it using a five-factor balancing test from People v. Taranovich, not a countdown clock, and it's a completely different right than the statutory CPL 30.30 deadlines that actually get cases dismissed on paper.
