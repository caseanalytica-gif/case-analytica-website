"I want my day in court, judged by 12." People say some version of that assuming there's a clock counting down to a dismissal. There is a right to a speedy trial in New York. It just doesn't work anything like that, and the five-factor balancing test courts actually use is a lot harder to win than the phrase makes it sound.
