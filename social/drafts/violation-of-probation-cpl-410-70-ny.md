Probation gets sold in the plea conversation as the outcome that avoids prison. It does, right up until someone decides it didn't work out. The hearing that decides that question runs on a lower burden of proof, with no jury, and the sentence waiting on the other side of a loss can be the maximum the original charge allowed.
