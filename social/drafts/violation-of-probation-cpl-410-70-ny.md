Probation gets sold as the deal that keeps you out of prison. CPL 410.70 lets a judge revoke it later with no jury and a lower burden of proof, preponderance of the evidence, not beyond a reasonable doubt. What's actually waiting on the other side of that hearing is the maximum sentence the original charge allowed.
