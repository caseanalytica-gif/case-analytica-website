Probation gets sold in the plea conversation as the outcome that avoids prison. It does, until CPL 410.70 gets triggered: a summary hearing, decided by a judge alone with no jury, on preponderance of the evidence instead of the standard that applied to the original charge. Lose it, and the maximum sentence from the original conviction comes back on the table.
