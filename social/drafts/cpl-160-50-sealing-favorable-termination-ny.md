Your case gets dismissed or you're acquitted, and in New York the arrest record gets sealed automatically. No petition, no motion, no judge to ask. Almost nobody is ever told that, and sealed doesn't mean erased: outside certain marijuana convictions, New York has no law that expunges a criminal record at all.
