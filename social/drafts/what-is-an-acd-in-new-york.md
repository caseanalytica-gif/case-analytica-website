If a case ends in an ACD, most people hear the initials, sign whatever they're handed, and walk out relieved without anyone explaining what just happened. An Adjournment in Contemplation of Dismissal isn't a conviction and it isn't an admission of guilt, but it comes with conditions that matter. Complete the adjournment period clean and the case is dismissed and sealed; miss it, and the case comes right back.
