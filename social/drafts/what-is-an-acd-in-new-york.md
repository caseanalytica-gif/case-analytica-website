An ACD isn't a conviction, and it isn't an admission of guilt. Stay clean for six months, or a year on a family offense, under CPL 170.55, and the case gets dismissed by operation of law and sealed. It isn't automatic though. Someone has to ask for it before a plea gets locked in.
