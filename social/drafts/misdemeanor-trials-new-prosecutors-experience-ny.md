## LinkedIn

**In 2019, 3 in 1,000 New York City misdemeanors reached a trial. Queens says new prosecutors get misdemeanor trials as part of the job.**

---

A New York public defender says young prosecutors take low-level misdemeanors all the way to trial to get experience, and that the person charged pays for it. We tested that against the public record.

The Queens District Attorney's fall 2023 entry-level posting promises new prosecutors "misdemeanor hearings and trials." The Center for Court Innovation found that 0.3 percent of New York City misdemeanors in 2019 were resolved at trial. The Brennan Center found that a misdemeanor conviction cuts annual earnings by an average of 16 percent.

Our conclusion: the practice is built into how offices train, a trial is rare enough to be a choice, and the cost lands on the person charged. What the record cannot show is why any one case was tried. Offices don't log that, and only the offices hold the count of how often it happens.

The article has the video, the sources and the question to put to your lawyer before a trial date.
