Probation and parole get treated like the same word by people who've never had to sit through either hearing. They're not the same system. Violate probation and a judge who never left the courthouse decides your fate. Violate parole and it's the New York State Board of Parole, moving under a completely different law, with its own hearings, its own deadlines, and its own burden of proof. Confuse the two and you'll walk into the wrong process not knowing what's actually being decided that day.
