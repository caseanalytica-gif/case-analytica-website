Violate probation and a judge who never left the courthouse decides your fate. Violate parole and it's the Board of Parole, moving under a completely different law with its own hearings and its own clock. The bar starts at probable cause within 10 days and climbs to clear and convincing evidence by the 45-day final hearing, a materially higher standard than what got the case there in the first place.
