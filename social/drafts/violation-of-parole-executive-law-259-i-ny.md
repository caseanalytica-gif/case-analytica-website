A parole violation warrant gets someone in front of a judge within 24 hours. It does not, by itself, guarantee they stay in custody. The law puts the burden on the state to justify continued detention, not on the person to justify release. Is that distinction actually being honored at the hearing, or is the warrant being treated like a done deal?
