If a stop turned into an arrest and a case, ask your attorney directly, early, and before any plea discussion: what reasonable suspicion did the officer articulate for the stop, and is it in the paperwork. A stop that cannot be justified on the record can take the evidence with it, but nobody will raise this for you if you do not ask.
