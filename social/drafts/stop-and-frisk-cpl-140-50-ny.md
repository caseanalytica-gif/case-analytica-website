A federal judge found the NYPD had adopted "a policy of indirect racial profiling by targeting racially defined groups for stops and frisks." That's not an advocacy group's characterization, that's the finding in Floyd v. City of New York after a nine-week trial. The statute still stands. Reasonable suspicion still has to be real, stop by stop.
