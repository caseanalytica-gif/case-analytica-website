Reasonable suspicion is a specific legal standard, not a phrase that excuses any stop. CPL 140.50 actually requires two separate findings, one to stop you and a second one to frisk you. A federal judge found the NYPD had collapsed that distinction into a pattern of suspicionless stops in Floyd v. City of New York.
