NYC's Mayor's Office of Criminal Justice committed $6.5 million in 2024 to 16 organizations running restorative justice circles across all five boroughs, for students, domestic violence survivors, people in addiction recovery, and teens arrested on gun charges. This is not a pilot program. It is part of a five-year plan to build community-based alternatives as the city works toward closing Rikers Island.
