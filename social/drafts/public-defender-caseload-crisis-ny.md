A public defender is a real licensed attorney: same bar exam, same law degree as anyone in private practice. What rarely gets said is what their caseload actually looks like. New York's own standard caps a full public defense workload at under 300 weighted cases a year, which works out to less than seven hours per case from the day it opens to the day it closes. In some counties, a single attorney carries more than double that. Same qualifications on paper. Wildly different amount of time for your case.
