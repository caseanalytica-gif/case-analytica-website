New York's own standards say a public defender shouldn't carry more than roughly 300 weighted cases a year. In parts of the state, a single public defender carries well over a hundred active cases at once anyway. Run 300 cases against a standard 2,000-hour work year and it comes out to under seven hours per case, start to finish.
