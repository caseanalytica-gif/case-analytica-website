Short answer: if a New York conviction hasn't been sealed yet under the Clean Slate Act, it can show up on a background check and cost you a job over something from years ago. The sealing clock is 3 years for a misdemeanor and 8 years for a felony, counted from release from incarceration, not from the arrest.
