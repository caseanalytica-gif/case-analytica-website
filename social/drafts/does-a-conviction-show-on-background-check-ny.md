A conviction that hasn't been sealed shows up on a standard background check in New York. Under the Clean Slate Act, sealing kicks in 3 years after sentence completion for a misdemeanor, 8 years for a felony, and the clock only starts once you're fully off parole or probation, not from the date of arrest.
