Civil immigration arrests at New York state courthouses jumped 1,200 percent in three years after federal guidance changed in 2017, according to the case record behind a November 2025 federal ruling. New York passed a law to stop it: ICE needs an actual judicial warrant, signed by a judge, not an administrative one signed by a supervisor, to make a civil arrest at a state courthouse. A federal judge just upheld that law, in writing.
