If ICE agents approach someone at or near a New York state courthouse, the only question that matters is this: do you have a judicial warrant, signed by a judge, and can I see it. An administrative warrant signed by an ICE supervisor does not count under New York's Protect Our Courts Act, and a federal judge upheld the law in November 2025.
