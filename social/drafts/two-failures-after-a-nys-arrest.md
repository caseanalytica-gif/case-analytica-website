Two things go wrong after a New York arrest, and neither shows up on the charging document: nobody explains what's actually happening, and nobody guarantees you someone with the time to fight for you. In immigration court, where there's no right to a free lawyer, self-represented people win their case about 4% of the time. With a lawyer, that jumps to roughly 48%.
