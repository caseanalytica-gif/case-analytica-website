Two things go wrong at almost the exact same moment after someone is arrested in New York, and neither one shows up on any charging document. Nobody sits the person down and explains what's actually happening to them. And the law doesn't guarantee anyone with the time or resources to actually fight for the best outcome, only someone with a law degree. That gap between those two guarantees is where most of the damage happens.
