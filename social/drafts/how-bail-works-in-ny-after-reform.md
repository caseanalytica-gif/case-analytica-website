Bail reform in New York gets talked about like it's one fixed rule from 2019 that never changed. It isn't. What's actually eligible for cash bail today is narrower than before 2020, but wider than the original 2019 reform by itself, because the law has been amended twice since, in 2020 and again in 2022.
