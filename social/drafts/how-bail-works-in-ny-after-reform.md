Bail reform gets talked about like it's one fixed rule from 2019. It isn't. The list of bail-eligible charges got expanded again in 2020 and 2022, and judges are required to state their reasoning for a bail amount on the record. If it wasn't clearly explained, that's something your attorney can ask for.
