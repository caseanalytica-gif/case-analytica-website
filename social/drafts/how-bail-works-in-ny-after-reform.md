Bail reform in New York did not get rid of cash bail. It narrowed it, then the list of exceptions grew twice, in 2020 and 2022. Whether a charge is bail-eligible today depends on which version of the law you are reading, and if bail does get set, you are entitled to ask the judge on the record why that number and not another.
