Ask someone what they were sentenced to and a lot of the time they'll say probation when the paperwork actually says conditional discharge, or the reverse. Both sound like the good outcome in a courtroom: no prison. They are not the same sentence. Only one puts a probation officer in your life, checking in on you, for years.
