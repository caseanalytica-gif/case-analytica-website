A finding of unfit to stand trial in New York doesn't end a case. It pauses it, and the custody that follows can run up to two-thirds of the maximum sentence the top felony charge carries, longer than a plea or even a conviction might have taken. Families hear "he's getting evaluated" and assume that's the soft outcome. Sometimes it isn't.
