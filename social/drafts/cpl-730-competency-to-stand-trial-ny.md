A case doesn't disappear because someone is found unfit to stand trial. It pauses. For a lot of families that pause turns into more time in psychiatric custody than the underlying charge would have carried on a straight conviction, because New York can hold a person under this statute for up to two-thirds of the maximum sentence the top charge allows.
