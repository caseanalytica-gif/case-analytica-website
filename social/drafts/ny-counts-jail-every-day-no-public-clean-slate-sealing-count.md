**New York counted 6,665 people in its jails this morning. I can't find a count of the records it has sealed.**

---

The city publishes who is in its jails every day. The state set itself a deadline of November 16, 2027 to seal every eligible old conviction under the Clean Slate Act, and I have not found a published count of how many it has sealed so far.

The last figure I found is more than 19 months old. In March 2025, a DCJS spokesperson told Spectrum News the court system had not yet sent the department any records to seal.

On October 31 I sent records requests to DCJS and the court system. DCJS logged mine today and gave itself about 20 business days. Whatever comes back, a number or a written statement that no record exists, I will publish it and add it to the Case Analytica data hub.

The piece also covers Rikers. The legal closure date is August 31, 2027. The four replacement jails will hold 4,160 people, according to Straight Arrow News, and the city counted 6,665 today.
