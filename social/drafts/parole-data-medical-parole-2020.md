## LinkedIn

**DOCCS approved 18 of the 1,049 medical parole requests it received in 2020.**

---

In 2020, as Covid spread through the prison system, DOCCS received 1,049 requests for medical parole. It approved 18. New York Focus reported the numbers in April 2022.

A DOCCS spokesperson told Bolts and New York Focus that many of those applicants may not have been medically eligible. That is part of the record too, and it is on the page.

The next year, applications dropped to 82. Of those, 10 people were released.

Case Analytica keeps these figures in one place, each with its period, its population and the source it came from, so you can check every number yourself.
