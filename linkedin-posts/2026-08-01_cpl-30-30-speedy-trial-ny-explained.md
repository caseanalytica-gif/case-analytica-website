## Post copy

Most people think CPL 30.30 guarantees a trial by a deadline in New York. It doesn't — it sets a deadline for prosecutors to *say they're ready*. Those are two very different things, and the gap between them is where cases quietly drag on for over a year.

The clock also stops for reasons most defendants never hear about — pretrial motions, competency proceedings, defense delays — so the real deadline is rarely the one on the calendar.

Ask your attorney: "What's the actual CPL 30.30 date on my case, after excludable time, and can I see how you calculated it?"
