Republish this reporting for free

News outlets, nonprofits and community groups can republish Case Analytica articles at no cost, under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International license (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). The terms below explain what that means here.

What you need to do

  • Keep the byline: Dean Mustaphalli, Case Analytica.
  • At the top or bottom, add: “This article was originally published by Case Analytica,” with a link to the original article.
  • Keep the links to sources, or the Sources list at the end. They are how a reader checks the article.
  • Keep any correction or update note, and keep the line saying the article is general information, not legal advice.

What you can change

  • You can edit for your house style, trim for length, and update time references such as “last week” to a date.
  • You can’t change the meaning, alter quotes, or add reporting of your own inside the article. If you add context, set it apart and label it as yours.

What is not included

  • Photos, video and graphics are not covered unless the credit line says Case Analytica. Images credited to someone else belong to them.
  • Running an article on a site that carries advertising is fine. Selling the article itself, or putting it behind a paywall on its own, is not.
  • Republishing does not mean Case Analytica endorses your publication, or that you endorse this reporting.

Let us know

Email caseanalytica@gmail.com with where the article ran. If that article is later corrected, you’ll get the correction.
