Parole file worksheet

Print it and mail it in. It follows the five steps in Read the Whole Parole File Before the Board Does.

What goes on this paper

Only what is written on the documents: who wrote each one and the date on it. Do not write an account of the offense here. That belongs in a conversation with a lawyer.

Interview date   Date I asked to see the file

Step 1: The portfolio, built first

These follow the categories in Executive Law § 259-i(2)(c)(A). Build this well ahead of the interview. Check off what you have a copy of.

  • ☐ Program completion certificates and program goals met
  • ☐ Therapy or treatment completed
  • ☐ Education: diploma, transcripts, college credits
  • ☐ Vocational training certificates
  • ☐ Work assignments and evaluations
  • ☐ Release plan: housing letter naming the address
  • ☐ Release plan: job offer or employment program
  • ☐ Release plan: support services lined up (program, mentor, reentry organization)
  • ☐ Letters of support, each dated and saying what the writer is offering
  • ☐ Everything sent to the SORC at my facility ahead of the interview, with a copy kept

Date the portfolio went to the SORC

Step 2: Ask for the whole file

  • ☐ Written request sent to my ORC to see the final file going to the Board (9 NYCRR 8000.5(c))
  • ☐ Request lists my name, ID number, the kind of hearing, and my facility (8000.5(c)(8))
  • ☐ Asked the ORC whether what they showed me is the full case record

Step 3: Learn every document and its date

One line per document. Mark whether it was written at or before sentencing, or since. You will not have a pen or paper during the review. Memorize who wrote each document and its date, and fill this in right after.

Step 4: What I want on the record

Write the dates you will ask the commissioner to confirm, and the points about your release plan you will state.

Step 5: If release is denied

The deadlines come from 9 NYCRR 8006.1 and 8006.2.

  • ☐ Date I received the written decision
  • ☐ Notice of appeal filed (due within 30 days of receiving the decision)
  • ☐ Transcript requested when I filed the notice
  • ☐ Appeal perfected (due within four months of filing the notice)
  • ☐ If the appeal wins: read who the decision says will hold the new interview

Notices of appeal go to the New York State Board of Parole, Appeals Unit, The Harriman State Campus, Albany, NY 12226 (9 NYCRR 8006.1(c)).
