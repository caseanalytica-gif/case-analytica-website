Reporter · Research Analyst

Dean Mustaphalli

Founder, reporter and research analyst, Case Analytica. Covers New York’s criminal courts, prisons and parole.

Case Analytica is Dean Mustaphalli. Before any of this, I spent years in finance, working at a top-tier Wall Street firm and later running my own investment fund. That career ended with a felony conviction, and I served time in New York State prison.

While I was incarcerated, I ran a transition program: helping other men get through their programs, prepare for parole board hearings, and work on their appeals in the law library. I won my own parole board appeal and came home in December 2023.
